The strategic story in one line: Payward is turning Kraken from a crypto exchange into a licensed, vertically integrated financial infrastructure group (bank, derivatives clearing, payments, tokenisation), with acquisitions buying licences as much as technology. Reap is the first big Asia move under that plan.

Your fit: lead with these

  • Tokenised securities = securities. xStocks are the role's first-named product. Prospectus, disclosure, offering restrictions and selling-restriction analysis are DCM core skills. Frame xStocks distribution in APAC as a securities-offering problem you've done a hundred times.
  • Hong Kong qualified + Mandarin. Native to the SFC/HKMA world, and you can work in Chinese-speaking markets (HK, Taiwan, plus counterpart dealings) without translation layers.
  • Structured products and derivatives exposure for OTC options and wholesale derivatives (keep it to what you've actually done; the same narrative you built for the FICC applications).
  • Managing local counsel across many jurisdictions is what DCM programme work is: selling restrictions across 20+ markets, cost control, scoping.
  • AI-assisted drafting. The JD asks for it, and you built a document classification pipeline. Mention briefly.

APAC product-legal issues to have a view on

  • Tokenised equities (xStocks): in HK, tokenised securities are securities under the SFO, so retail distribution triggers offering rules and the SFC's tokenised-product circulars. Singapore has MAS SFA prospectus and capital markets licensing. Most of APAC needs a professional/accredited-investor-only route first.
  • OTC options and leverage: retail leverage caps and bans (Japan's low crypto leverage cap, Australia's DDO and product intervention orders, HK SFC limits on VATP derivatives for retail).
  • Stablecoins: HK Stablecoins Ordinance (in force Aug 2025, HKMA licensing for issuers and offer restrictions). MAS single-currency stablecoin framework. Japan's EPI regime. Relevant for Reap's card and payment flows.
  • Staking and earn: HK SFC has allowed staking for licensed VATPs under conditions. Singapore restricts retail staking/lending for DPT services. Australia is moving under the new platform regime.
  • Marketing and finfluencers: SFC/MAS restrictions on retail crypto marketing, Singapore's ban on public-area DPT ads, ASIC finfluencer guidance.
  • Licensing sequencing: HK (SFC VATP plus AMLO; heavy, slow, retail allowed), SG (MAS DTSP and MPI; strict on overseas-only business since June 2025), JP (FSA registration plus JVCEA; Kraken has left twice). Reap gives a ready-made HK/SG payments platform to build from.
  • Lesson from ASIC v Bit Trade: a $8M penalty for offering a margin extension product without a target market determination. Product governance is the core of the job; worth referencing constructively.

These regime summaries are high level and current to my knowledge. Check the latest SFC/MAS/FSA positions before the interview, especially on staking, stablecoins and Singapore's DTSP rules.
